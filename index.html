Technically Alive

An animated YouTube series about real science and real survival scenarios. Every episode drops you into an extreme situation — stranded on the Moon, lost at sea, at the bottom of the ocean — and works out, with real science, whether you'd survive.

Watch on YouTube: @TechnicallyAliveTV
