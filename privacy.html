Privacy Policy

Technically Alive Uploader · last updated 9 November 2026

Technically Alive Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Technically Alive to upload that channel's own videos.

What data the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, captions and thumbnails to the owner's channel and to read the status and public statistics of those videos.

How the data is stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never sent anywhere except to Google. The app has no server and no database. It does not collect, store or share any data about other people.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
